Our Mission

Glasses restore sight. The Gospel gives life.

Our mission is to make the Gospel clear to those in need — by providing prescription glasses and sharing the hope of Jesus Christ.

Mission statement

Vision for the Gospel, Inc. exists to meet the physical need of poor eyesight and the spiritual need of every person. We provide vision services and glasses to those in need, and we do it side by side with local Christians who share the Gospel with the people they serve.

Our programs and services

Vision screeningsCommunity screenings that identify people who need glasses.
Eye examsExams by licensed optometrists to determine each person’s prescription.
Prescription glassesReal prescription glasses, fitted and given free of charge.
Gospel outreachLocal Christians present the Gospel and invite follow-up with a local church.

Why glasses?

Prescription glasses open a door — to study, to work, to read. A child who can finally see the blackboard, or a grandmother who can read Scripture again, has received a real gift. We walk through that door with the best news a person will ever hear.

How your gift helps
